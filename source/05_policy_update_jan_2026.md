# Policy Update: January 2026

Memo to all staff | From: Aoife Byrne, owner | Date: 15 January 2026

Thank you all for a great 2025. Following the staff meeting in December, I am making the changes below. **Where this memo differs from the Staff Handbook (2025 edition) or the Purchasing and Supplier Policy (2025 edition), this memo applies.**

## 1. Staff discount (from 1 February 2026)

The staff discount increases from 20% to 25% on food and drink when you are not on shift. The discount for an accompanying guest is discontinued. Retail coffee beans and gift cards are still excluded.

## 2. Sick pay (from 1 February 2026)

The first 5 sick days in each leave year will now be paid at 100% of your normal daily pay, with no daily cap. The rules on phoning in and medical certificates are unchanged.

## 3. Sunday opening hours (from 1 March 2026)

On Sundays the café will open from 09:00 to 16:00, one hour later than before, because of the demand we saw last summer.

## 4. Rota publication (from 1 February 2026)

The rota for the following week will be published every Wednesday by 17:00 instead of Thursday, to give everyone more notice.

## 5. Café manager spending limit (from 1 February 2026)

The café manager can now approve orders up to €3,000 per order (previously €2,500). The shift lead limit of €500 is unchanged.

Everything else in the Staff Handbook and the Purchasing and Supplier Policy stays the same. If you have any questions, please talk to me or to Ciarán.

Aoife
