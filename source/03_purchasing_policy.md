# Purchasing and Supplier Policy

Kildare Craft Coffee Ltd | 2025 edition | Approved by Aoife Byrne, owner

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains who can buy what, from whom, and how supplier invoices are handled. It protects the business from overspending, fraud and duplicate payments.

## 2. Approved suppliers

We only order from the approved suppliers below. To add a new supplier, see section 4.

| Supplier | What we buy | Ordering |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bean & Leaf Importers Ltd | Coffee beans | Order by Tuesday 12:00 for Thursday delivery |
| Leinster Dairy Co-op | Milk, oat milk, cream | Order by 14:00 for delivery the next morning (Monday to Saturday) |

| Kaffeerösterei Schmidt GmbH | Guest coffees from Germany | Monthly, by email |

## 3. Who can approve spending

Only the roles below may place orders, and only with approved suppliers. Baristas may not place orders.

| Role | Can approve orders up to |
| --- | --- |
| Shift lead | €500 per order |
| Café manager | €2,500 per order |
| Owner | Any amount |

Any single purchase over €1,000 that is not a routine stock order needs two written quotes before it is approved.

## 4. New suppliers

A new supplier can only be used after the owner has approved it in writing. The owner checks the supplier's company registration, bank details and, for food suppliers, their food safety registration.

## 5. Supplier invoices

- Suppliers email invoices to invoices@kildarecraftcoffee.ie. Invoices sent anywhere else must be forwarded to that address.
- Invoices are logged automatically when they arrive. Duplicate invoices, invoices from suppliers not on the approved list, and invoices over €10,000 are sent to the owner for approval before they can be paid.
- Invoices are paid by bank transfer in the weekly payment run every Wednesday, on or before their due date.
- Never change a supplier's bank details because of an email request. Phone the supplier on the number we already hold to confirm any change first. This is a common fraud.

## 6. Receiving deliveries

- Check the delivery against the delivery note: count the items and check for damage.
- Check chilled deliveries with the probe thermometer. **Reject any chilled delivery above 8°C.** Deliveries between 5°C and 8°C may be accepted only if they go into the fridge immediately; note the temperature on the delivery note.
- Sign the delivery note, write any shortages or rejected items on it, and photograph it.
- Put chilled and frozen goods away first.

## 7. Returns and credit notes

Report damaged, missing or rejected items to the supplier on the same day and ask for a credit note. Give the credit note to the café manager, who checks it against the next invoice.
