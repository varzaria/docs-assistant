# Equipment Guide

Kildare Craft Coffee Ltd | Version 2, June 2025 | For baristas and shift leads

## 1. Espresso machine

Our espresso machine is a two-group commercial machine. Only baristas who have been signed off by the café manager may use it unsupervised.

### Daily start-up

1. Switch the machine on at least 30 minutes before opening so that it reaches temperature.
2. Check the boiler pressure gauge: it should read between 1.0 and 1.5 bar.
3. Flush each group head for 5 seconds.
4. Pull two test shots and taste them before serving customers.

### Our espresso recipe

| Measure | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Dose (ground coffee in) | 18 g |
| Yield (espresso out) | 36 g |
| Extraction time | 27 to 32 seconds |

- If the shot runs faster than 27 seconds, adjust the grinder one step finer.
- If it runs slower than 32 seconds, adjust the grinder one step coarser.
- Weigh every shot during the first hour of each day and after any grinder adjustment.

### Daily close-down

1. Backflush each group head with espresso machine detergent: 5 cycles of 10 seconds on and 10 seconds off, then 5 cycles with clean water.
2. Soak the portafilters and baskets in detergent solution for 20 minutes, then rinse.
3. Purge and wipe the steam wands, and soak the steam tips if they are blocked.
4. Wipe the drip trays and the outside of the machine.

## 2. Grinders

We have two grinders: one for the house blend and one for the guest coffee. Empty and brush the hoppers at close every day. Check the grind each morning with the first test shots.

## 3. Water filter

The water filter protects the machine from limescale. It must be changed every 3 months, or after 5,000 litres, whichever comes first. The date of the last change is written on the label on the filter housing. Eireann Espresso Services changes the filter during the quarterly service.

## 4. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No water from the group head | Check that the water supply valve under the counter is fully open. |
| Steam wand weak | Purge the wand; check that the boiler pressure is between 1.0 and 1.5 bar. |
| Shots running very fast or very slow | Adjust the grind (see the espresso recipe). |
| Machine leaking water | Switch the machine off at the wall, put down a wet floor sign, and phone Eireann Espresso Services. |
| Error light flashing | Switch off for 1 minute and restart. If the light is still flashing, phone Eireann Espresso Services. |

Never open the machine's casing or try to repair it yourself.

## 6. Dishwasher

The dishwasher's final rinse must reach 82°C. Check the temperature display at the start of each day and write it on the cleaning record. If the rinse is below 82°C, wash cups and plates by hand and tell the café manager.
